MochiMochi Alarm — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-01
Developer: Hsuan Yu Liu
Contact: lovesnoopy.world@gmail.com

Summary

MochiMochi Alarm has no server of its own and no account. Alarms, holiday settings, and the photos you pick for the frame stay on your iPhone. The app does not operate analytics or behavioral tracking. Ads come from Google AdMob, are always non-personalized, do not use App Tracking Transparency, and do not read the advertising identifier.

1. Where your data lives

  • Alarm times, repeats, ringtones, holiday exceptions, and the travel reminder stay in the app on this iPhone.
  • Frame photos are ones you picked with the system picker and copied into the app's documents. The app does not read your whole photo library.
  • Preferences such as language and US state stay in local settings on this phone.
  • None of this is uploaded to our server. We do not have one.

2. What we process, and why

DataPurposeBasis

We do not collect diagnostics, crash reports, or usage analytics ourselves, and we do not collect location. The ads SDK may process device, advertising, and interaction data under Google's policy. See section 3A.

3. Holiday data

The Taiwan, Japan, and United States holiday lists are built into the app. While you use it, the phone does not contact the Taiwan DGPA, the Japan Cabinet Office, or Nager.Date. If a Taiwan override URL was set at build time, the app may fetch one small difference file when it opens. That file has no account and no photos. With no URL, it does not make that request. The Japan and United States lists never accept that file.

3A. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Formats: a banner at the bottom of the alarm list. An interstitial can appear only after a ring or snooze has ended and you are back on the list, at most once per foreground entry, and not again for at least 60 minutes.
  • Never shown while ringing, during snooze, in the photo frame, or on the Lock Screen.
  • No rewarded ads, and no purchase or subscription to remove ads.
  • Tracking: no App Tracking Transparency and no advertising identifier. Every request is non-personalized, and the Publisher First-Party ID is off.
  • Consent: in some regions Google may show its own consent form, in the phone's language. If an ad does not load, alarms still work.
  • Processing: ads are loaded by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. See Google's iOS data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your alarms or photos because of an ad.

4. Frame photos

A photo is copied into the app only after you pick it. Deleting the app, or removing the photo in the app, removes that copy. We do not upload photos, and we do not write them back to your photo library.

5. Your choices

  • You can change or delete alarms, photos, and the language in the app.
  • Deleting the app deletes the alarms and photos stored in it.
  • There is no account, so we cannot delete data from a server for you.

6. Permissions

  • Photos: the app does not ask for access to your whole library. The frame uses the system picker and copies only the photos you choose.
  • Alarms or notifications: so the alarm can ring.
  • Focus status: only so the frame can follow the system auto-lock while a Focus is on.

7. Retention

Data stays until you delete it in the app, or until you delete the app.

8. Children

MochiMochi Alarm is a general utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Third parties

  • Apple: ringing uses iOS alarms or notifications. The frame uses photos you picked. iOS handles those on your device.
  • Google AdMob: see section 3A.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date on this page changes.

11. Contact

This page is both the privacy policy and the support page. For privacy or support questions, email lovesnoopy.world@gmail.com.
