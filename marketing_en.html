Let tomorrow's alarm make sense.

MochiMochi Alarm keeps your weekly routine, named public holidays, and one-off changes separate, so a long weekend does not mean hunting for an off switch.

◌

Skip named public holidays

It follows the Taiwan, Japan, or United States holiday calendar selected in the app. Any alarm can still be set to ring on holidays.

↗

Ask the night before

Before a holiday or a make-up workday, choose whether to skip or add one alarm. Your choice stays on this iPhone.

▣

Change only one day

Editing a day from the calendar preserves the original weekly rule and creates an exception only for that date.

Details that belong by the bed.

From sounds to display preferences, the important controls stay quiet and close at hand.

01

Sound and snooze

Choose a bundled sound, a custom audio file, or vibration only, then set a snooze duration and limit.

02

Holiday calendars follow language

Traditional Chinese uses Taiwan, Japanese uses Japan, and English (US) uses the United States. English can also select a state.

03

Made for iPhone

iOS 26.1 and later use AlarmKit. Earlier systems still receive local alarm notifications. Dates and times follow your device time zone.

Your alarms and photos stay on your iPhone.

MochiMochi Alarm has no account and no server. It copies only photos you select, while alarms, exceptions, travel reminders, and preferences remain on device. Ads are non-personalized and never appear while ringing, snoozing, in the photo frame, or on the lock screen.
